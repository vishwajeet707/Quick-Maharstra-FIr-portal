Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

F.I.R. / N.C. / COMPLAINT

1. What is an F.I.R?

As per Section 154 of Cr.P.C. 1973, F.I.R. (First Information Report) is a report made to the police about the commission of a cognizable offence and registered by the police.

2. How do I lodge an F.I.R.?

The informant/complainant should go to the police station having jurisdiction over the area where the offence is committed and report it to the officer in charge. If information is given over the phone, the complainant should visit the police station later for F.I.R. registration.

3. What is a Non-Cognizable (NC) Offence?

As per Section 155 of Cr.P.C. 1973, a Non-Cognizable offence is one where the police cannot investigate without prior permission from a Magistrate.

4. How do I lodge an NC complaint?

NC complaints are reported in the same manner as F.I.R.s. The officer-in-charge will record the complaint in writing and provide a free copy to the complainant.

5. What is meant by a public place?

A public place includes areas such as the foreshore, precincts of public buildings or monuments, and places accessible for drawing water, washing, bathing, or recreation. {B.P. Act 1951, sec 2(13)}.
